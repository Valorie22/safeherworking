Data model

Grouped by concern. The two tables marked append-only are enforced as such by triggers in 0001_schema.sql — an UPDATE or DELETE on incident_events raises an exception, so the timeline cannot be rewritten after the fact by anyone, including a developer with a psql prompt.

One pipeline, two branches, converging on resolution. Note that a medical SOS assigns a station as well as a hospital — a medical emergency may still need police, and a road accident always does. Cross-dispatch works in both directions.
